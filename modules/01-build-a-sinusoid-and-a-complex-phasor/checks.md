# Checks

## Observe

1. Point to the plot that directly shows amplitude as phasor radius.
2. State the rotation direction of positive and negative complex frequency.
3. Identify which change moves the starting point without changing the number of cycles.

## Interpret

- Explain why `real(z)` equals the real cosine.
- Explain why increasing frequency changes rotation rate but not IQ-circle radius.
- Explain why the broken case can display a plausible but incorrect lower-frequency waveform.

## Teach-back completion

In two or three sentences, answer the guiding question: **How do amplitude, frequency, and phase appear in time and in the complex plane?**

A satisfactory answer must mention radius, rotation rate, and starting angle, and must connect the real cosine to the I-axis projection.
