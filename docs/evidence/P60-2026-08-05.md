## Deterministic oracle results

The independent oracle uses only the Python standard library and separately
implements matrix arithmetic, both motion models, Joseph covariance updates,
interaction, log-domain likelihood weights, combination, scoring, and input
validation.

- Seed 6007 begins with standard-normal values
  `(1.4847920069867884, 1.9773858803013205, 0.3100537935711487, 0.26093500376378875)`.
- Reviewed mean maneuver-mode probability is `0.5175913403309493` on
  maneuver scans and `0.34768296548420047` on straight scans.
- Reviewed IMM overall position RMSE is `9.194530412939912 m`; it is lower
  than the fixed straight model's overall RMSE, and its maneuver-scan RMSE is
  lower than the fixed model's maneuver-scan RMSE.
- Maneuver-mode mean probability increases across the reviewed acceleration
  sweep, and IMM maneuver RMSE stays below fixed-model maneuver RMSE in all
  three cases.
- Dominant-mode switch counts are `[12, 6, 4]` as symmetric stay probability
  changes through `[0.80, 0.94, 0.99]`.
- The broken mode probability is exactly zero at every scan. The broken
  combined state exactly equals the fixed straight-filter state. Restored
  state and probability histories exactly equal the reviewed IMM histories.
- With zero initial maneuver probability but the reviewed nonzero transition
  support, maneuver probability becomes positive on the first scan and later
  exceeds `0.5`; the result no longer equals the fixed straight filter. This
  isolates transition-based mode reentry from full baseline reinitialization.
- At every scan, the oracle's combined covariance equals the weighted sum of
  conditioned covariance and between-model outer-product spread, remains
  symmetric, and is not reduced below the within-model diagonal. The reviewed
  record has nontrivial model-disagreement spread rather than a vacuous equality.

These are static/simulated oracle results, not MATLAB runtime results.

  normalization tolerances rather than oracle bit identity.

  effectiveness.
