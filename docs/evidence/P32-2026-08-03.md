zero-extends an integer-delayed echo, correlates lag by lag, interpolates the
half-power crossings, and applies the same range conversion.

- Baseline peak: delay sample 640; peak magnitude 400 for 400 unit-amplitude
  samples; measured full -3 dB width 16.442479 m.
- Baseline scales: raw extent 1,498.962290 m; nominal `c/(2B)` 18.737029 m;
  sampled coherent gain `10log10(400)=26.020600` dB; `B*T` gain
  `10log10(80)=19.030900` dB.
- Bandwidth `[4 8 16]` MHz widths: `[32.957955 16.442479 8.202776]` m,
  strictly decreasing. Nominal scales are `[37.474057 18.737029 9.368514]` m.
- Duration `[5 10 20]` microseconds widths at 8 MHz:
  `[16.416208 16.442479 16.454858]` m, a spread below one 3.747406 m range
  sample. `B*T=[40 80 160]` and gains are `[16.020600 19.030900 22.041200]` dB.
- The `0.55B` mismatch peak is 78.454384 versus 400 matched, a -14.147104 dB
  loss on the shared recovered-peak reference; its measured width is
  581.931024 m versus 16.442479 m matched.
- Negative cases reject missing/empty/duplicate/malformed identity data,
