## Physical model and independent static oracle

The implemented model is:

```text
lambda = c/f_c
f_d = 2 v/lambda
Delta_phi = 2 pi f_d/PRF
H = 1 - exp(-j Delta_phi)
normalized_gain = |H|/2
v_k = k lambda PRF/2
combined_gain = max(gain_PRF1, gain_PRF2)
combined_detection = detection_PRF1 OR detection_PRF2
```

With `c=299792458 m/s`, `f_c=10 GHz`, PRF 1 `=4000 Hz`, and PRF 2 `=5300 Hz`, the independent oracle produced wavelength `0.0299792458 m`, first positive blind speeds `59.9584916000 m/s` and `79.4450013700 m/s`, and baseline Doppler `4000 Hz`. At the primary first blind speed, normalized gain is `0.000000000000` for PRF 1 and `0.696551029063` for PRF 2, above the illustrative `0.30` threshold. Both gains are zero at stationary velocity.

The fixed-target second-PRF sweep produced:

| 4200 | 0.149042266176 |

Complex-sequence tests independently applied adjacent-sample subtraction to 32 pulses. The primary blind sequence canceled below `1e-12`; the secondary sequence's measured RMS gain matched the analytic response to 12 decimal places. Nulls at integer multiples `k=-2..2` were checked for each PRF. The declared resource formula evaluates to 58,934 numeric values below the immutable 100,000 ceiling. These values are host-language/static evidence, not MATLAB results.
