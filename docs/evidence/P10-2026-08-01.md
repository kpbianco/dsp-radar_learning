## Expected figure and metric inventory

The source defines five figure groups. These are source-reviewed expectations,
not rendered evidence:

1. Original and decimated time signals in milliseconds/volts and spectra in
   hertz/dBV, including the 300 Hz new-Nyquist marker and 180 Hz alias.
2. Zero-inserted and reconstructed signals in milliseconds/volts and spectra
   in hertz/dBV, including the 510 Hz first image.
3. High-tone sweep `[220 280 340 420]` Hz versus observed low-rate frequency in
   hertz and fold-component amplitude in volts.
4. Reconstruction sweep `[9 17 33 65]` taps versus first-image amplitude in
   dBV and recovered 90 Hz amplitude in volts.
5. Broken decimation/interpolation paths versus their filtered recoveries.

The workspace `results` structure retains sample rates in samples/second,
frequencies in hertz, amplitudes in volts, suppression in decibels, sweep
vectors, seed, and comparison tolerance. The script writes no result file.

## Independent numerical contracts

`tests/test_p10_module.py` independently implements the 65-tap normalized
windowed-sinc FIR, aligned FIR sum, tone projection, frequency folding,
decimation, zero insertion, and reconstruction. The clean two-tone model
reported:

- 420 Hz folds to `180 Hz` at 600 samples/s.
- Naive alias amplitude is `0.650000000000 V`; prefiltering reduces it to
  `0.002291037372 V`, or `49.057623660 dB` suppression.
- Zero insertion places `0.249662270983 V` at both 90 Hz and the 510 Hz image.
- End-to-end filtered decimation/interpolation restores the 90 Hz tone to
  `0.997569901197 V`, while leaving `0.003137201145 V` at 180 Hz and
  `0.000739369648 V` at 510 Hz.
- Applying only the reconstruction FIR after naive decimation preserves the
  wanted 90 Hz tone at `0.997493924145 V` and removes the 420 Hz image to
  `0.000977024389 V`, but the already-folded 180 Hz component remains at
  `0.644451829156 V`. This behaviorally distinguishes removable images from
  irreversible aliasing.
- Reconstruction image suppression is `50.569825967 dB` in the independent
  clean model.
- The frequency sweep folds to `[220, 280, 260, 180] Hz`.
- The 510 Hz image amplitudes for `[9, 17, 33, 65]` taps are
  `[0.143900676250, 0.000888807758, 0.000862221046, 0.000739369648] V`.

These independent equations do not substitute for MATLAB syntax, graphics, or
runtime evidence.

   MATLAB compatibility. Ordinary `plot` segments now draw the same markers,

   the first scaffold now derive from the canonical manifest, so later batches
   can advance the shared frontier without invalidating retained P10 tests.

   experiment intentionally uses two isolated broken paths so each ordering
