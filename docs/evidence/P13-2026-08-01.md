## Independent numerical contracts

`tests/test_p13_module.py` independently evaluates direct finite sums without
calling MATLAB or parsing script output:

- an N-point DFT is embedded at every `factor`-th point of the factor-N padded
  DFT for factors 1, 4, and 16 to below `1e-12` complex-voltage error;
- a direct projection at 201.5 Hz agrees with the corresponding 2048-point
  dense-grid value to below `1e-12`;
- padding changes display spacing from 8 Hz to 2 Hz to 0.5 Hz while the
  128-sample Rayleigh interval remains 8 Hz and rectangular null-to-null width
  remains 16 Hz;
- increasing measured record length from 128 to 256 to 512 changes the
  Rayleigh interval from 8 Hz to 4 Hz to 2 Hz for the fixed 4 Hz-separated
  tones;
- the clean 128-sample response has one dominant peak and a positive
  midpoint-to-tone level, while the clean 512-sample response has two dominant
  peaks and a midpoint more than 200 dB below the true-tone projections;
- source review verifies the private 512-sample noise record is normalized to
  the requested RMS; the unchanged 128/256-sample prefixes retain their own
  reported realized RMS, which bounds each prefix projection through
  Cauchy-Schwarz. An independent 100-realization robustness probe retained the
  correct endpoint threshold and peak-count classifications in every trial;
- the broken 0.5 Hz display-grid claim predicts eight false "resolution
  cells," while the correct short/long separation ratios are 0.5 and 2.

These independent equations do not substitute for MATLAB syntax, private-seed
noise, graphics, or runtime evidence.

## Independent system-risk review repair

The post-implementation review found and repaired two material P13 defects:

1. **High — default MATLAB experiment stopped in the observation sweep.**
   `comparison_frequency_axis_hz >= ...` and `<= ...` each produce a logical
   vector, but `experiment.m` combined them with scalar short-circuit `&&`.
   MATLAB therefore cannot construct `search_mask`; the default run stops
   before peak classification, retained results, or any figure is produced.
   The repair parenthesizes both comparisons and combines them with elementwise
   `&`. A regression independently verifies that the intended inclusive
   190--210 Hz window contains 161 points on the 0.125 Hz grid and rejects the
   scalar operator in that assignment.
2. **Medium — a documented-invalid boundary passed early validation.** The
   source and independent validator admitted a tone separation exactly equal
   to the long-record Rayleigh interval, but the later recovery assertion
   required the separation ratio to exceed one. That edit consumed random,
   signal, and FFT work before failing. The early source and independent
   validators now require separation strictly above the long interval, matching
   the checks, recovery assertion, and existing evidence claim. A boundary
   regression verifies rejection before allocation.

The previously described 100-realization robustness probe is now a retained
behavioral test rather than an unsupported one-off claim. It independently
forms normalized complex noise with shared 128/512-sample prefixes and verifies
for 100 deterministic seeds that the short record keeps one dominant peak and
a positive midpoint level while the long record keeps two dominant peaks and a
midpoint below -20 dB. This is simulated host-language evidence, not MATLAB RNG
or runtime evidence.
