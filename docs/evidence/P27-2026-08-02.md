## Experiment and figure/metric inventory

All axes and metrics identify normalized units, decibels, probability, or
dimensionless counts.

1. **Matched-filter signal flow:** transmitted and received normalized pulse
   amplitude versus within-symbol sample index; empirical probability mass of
   matched-filter statistics for transmitted `-1` and `+1` versus normalized
   decision-statistic amplitude. Endpoint display bins absorb the unbounded
   Gaussian tails, and explicit conservation assertions require each
   conditional distribution to sum to one.
2. **Running Monte Carlo estimate:** Bernoulli error outcome versus independent
   trial index; running BER, 95% Wilson limits, and analytic BPSK/AWGN BER versus
   independent trial count.
3. **Empirical BER distribution:** probability mass of 40 non-overlapping
   100-independent-trial block BER values, with analytic BER marked.
4. **Controlled sweeps:** BER and Wilson interval versus prefix trial count;
   empirical and analytic BER versus `E_b/N_0` in dB using common random
   numbers and only rescaling noise.
5. **Broken reuse and recovery:** first 100 independent error outcomes against
   copies of one lucky outcome; independent-trial BER against the invalid
   repeated-trial report and analytic reference.

The deterministic source uses unit-energy BPSK, a 16-sample rectangular pulse,
4,000 independent symbol/noise trials, `E_b/N_0 = 2 dB`, and a real AWGN sample
standard deviation `sqrt(E_b/(2*10^(Eb/N0_dB/10)))`. The trial-count prefixes
are `[10,25,100,500,4000]`; the common-noise Eb/N0 sweep is
`[-4,-2,0,2,4] dB`. Wilson intervals use `z=1.96`. Forty 100-trial blocks form
the empirical BER distribution.

Retained `results` include controls; symbols, unit noise, pulse, matched-filter
statistics, decisions, outcomes, running BER and interval, final error count,
empirical/analytic BER, block BER, both distribution summaries, both sweep

count/invalid-independence marker/nominal interval, exact recovery status, and
resource bounds. Fixed ceilings are 4,000 trials, 16 samples per symbol, five
cases per sweep, five figure groups, and 700,000 conservatively counted numeric

correctness defect: finite histogram edges dropped Gaussian tail samples while
the plotted probabilities were normalized by the full class/trial count. A

assertions require exact count and probability-mass conservation for both
distributions, plot labels disclose the tail folding, and the new behavioral
test injects out-of-range samples to prove none are lost. MATLAB runtime and
