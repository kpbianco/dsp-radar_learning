the conjugate RF copy rather than producing no output. The implementation,
lesson, broken case, and independent model preserve that distinction. It also
made the real-input `A/2` mixer term and visible `2x` calibrated convention
explicit.

A Markdown punctuation expectation was corrected without changing content.

the independent malformed-control model now reject both edits before work.

The final independent system-risk review inspected the complete tracked and
