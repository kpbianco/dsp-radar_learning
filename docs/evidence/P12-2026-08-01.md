## Expected figure and metric inventory

The source defines four figure groups. These are source-reviewed expectations,
not rendered evidence:

1. **P12 clean leakage versus noise** — repeated-record time in milliseconds
   and in-phase amplitude in volts, plus clean/noisy rectangular spectra over
   frequency in hertz and magnitude relative to the tone in decibels.
2. **P12 window sweep** — the identical noiseless 138.8 Hz tone under
   rectangular, Hann, Hamming, Blackman, and flat-top windows, plotted in dBc.
3. **P12 window metrics** — -3 dB main-lobe width in hertz, N-bin peak error in
   decibels after coherent-gain correction, and maximum sidelobe level in dBc.
4. **P12 offset sweep and broken noise estimate** — rectangular spectra for
   offsets `[0, 0.20, 0.35, 0.50]` bin and true/broken/injected/recovered RMS
   amplitudes in volts.

The workspace `results` structure retains sample rate and bin spacing in hertz,
record duration in seconds, tone frequency in hertz, boundary jump and noise
RMS in volts, coherent gain, width in hertz/bins, amplitude error in decibels,
sidelobe level in dBc, off-peak energy fraction, and broken/recovered RMS in
volts. The experiment writes no result or figure file.

## Independent numerical contracts

`tests/test_p12_module.py` independently evaluates periodic window equations
and direct DFT sums without calling MATLAB or parsing script output:

- at 1024 samples/s and `N=128`, bin spacing is 8 Hz and bin 17.35 is 138.8 Hz;
- an exact-bin complex tone has off-peak energy fraction below numerical
  tolerance, while a half-bin tone has more than half its energy outside
  whichever single bin is largest;
- periodic coherent gains are exactly 1, 0.5, 0.54, 0.42, and 0.21557895 for
  rectangular, Hann, Hamming, Blackman, and the five-term flat-top formulas;
- the rectangular first sidelobe is independently bounded near -13 dBc, while
  Hann and Blackman progressively lower sidelobes and widen the main lobe;
- flat-top produces less N-bin peak-amplitude error at 0.35-bin offset than
  rectangular and stays within 0.02 dB of the input amplitude;
- circular -3 dB width and sidelobe metrics remain invariant when the same
  valid tone moves from bin 17.35 to bin 62.35 and its flat-top main lobe wraps
  across the Nyquist boundary;
- a Parseval-scaled nonpeak RMS for the noiseless 0.35-bin rectangular tone is
  over ten times the requested 0.02 V noise, proving the broken classifier can
  mistake deterministic leakage for noise.

These independent equations do not substitute for MATLAB syntax, seeded-noise,
graphics, or runtime evidence.

## Independent system-risk review repair

The independent review found one material correctness and missing-test defect
inside P12 scope. `tone_bin=62` is valid under the published `N=128` control
guard, but the flat-top main lobe wraps across the Nyquist boundary. The
original linear half-power search could fail to find its right crossing, and
the original linear sidelobe mask could classify the wrapped main lobe as a
sidelobe. The accepted control could therefore stop the lesson or report
physically wrong window tradeoffs.

invariance checks, then the test failed because the MATLAB experiment lacked
circular metric handling. The repair triplicates the bounded dense magnitude
vector for adjacent half-power crossing searches and uses wrapped frequency
distance for main-lobe exclusion. The same test then passed, and all 17 P12
