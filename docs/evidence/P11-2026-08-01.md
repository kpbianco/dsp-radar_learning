## Expected figure and metric inventory

The source defines four figure groups. These are source-reviewed expectations,
not rendered evidence:

1. **P11 baseline bin map** — complex I/Q time in milliseconds/volts,
   normalized magnitude by zero-based bin, centered signed frequency in hertz,
   and projection phase in radians above a 0.05 V validity threshold.
2. **P11 fractional-bin sweep** — normalized nearby-bin magnitudes for offsets
   `[0, 0.25, 0.50]` and the two neighbor magnitudes in volts.
3. **P11 record-length sweep** — a fixed 144 Hz tone for `N=[32,64,128]`,
   with normalized spectra, bin spacing, and nearest-bin reporting error in
   hertz.
4. **P11 broken axis and recovery** — identical normalized spectrum samples
   labeled first by the broken one-based frequency axis and then by recovered
   zero-based bins, both in hertz/volts.

The workspace `results` structure retains sample rate, record duration, bin
spacing, bin/index/frequency identity, normalized magnitude, phase, sweep
vectors, peak errors, seed, and broken/recovered label errors with units in the
field names. The experiment writes no result file.

## Independent numerical contracts

`tests/test_p11_module.py` independently evaluates the DFT equation without
calling MATLAB or parsing script outputs. For a unit complex tone with phase
0.35 rad at 1024 samples/s:

- `N=64` gives 16 Hz spacing; 144 Hz is zero-based bin 9, stored at MATLAB
  index 10, with ideal normalized magnitude 1 V and phase 0.35 rad.
- a 152 Hz half-bin tone gives equal ideal magnitudes
  `1/(64 sin(pi/128)) = 0.6366836927 V` in bins 9 and 10; their wrapped phase
  separation magnitude is `63*pi/64 = 3.0925052684 rad`.
- fixed 144 Hz lies at fractional/exact bins `[4.5, 9, 18]` for record lengths
  `[32,64,128]`, whose spacings are `[32,16,8]` Hz.
- using MATLAB index 10 as `k` reports 160 Hz; recovery with `k=index-1`
  reports 144 Hz, so the broken error is exactly one 16 Hz bin.

These equations do not substitute for MATLAB syntax, graphics, seeded-noise,
or runtime evidence.
