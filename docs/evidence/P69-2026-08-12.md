## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The permanent standard-library oracle reproduces the exact Park-Miller and
Box-Muller private stream, sampled centered-chirp phase, valid-overlap mixer,
Hann weighting, a full 65,536-point radix-2 FFT, the complete positive-spectrum
peak search, and log-power parabolic interpolation.
For `fs = 80 MHz`, `T = 40 us`, `B = 20 MHz`, `S = 0.5 THz/s`, `R = 45 m`,
echo voltage `0.70`, and noise RMS `0.01`:

- round-trip delay is `0.300 us`;
- theoretical beat is `150.000000 kHz`;
- deterministic FFT estimate is `150.005342 kHz`; and
- reconstructed range is `45.001603 m`.

The ideal noise-free sampled oracle independently verifies that every
successive mixer ratio equals `exp(j 2 pi f_b/fs)` to within `2e-11` and that
the chosen up-chirp mixer produces positive frequency.

For known ranges `15/30/45/60/75 m`, theoretical beats are
`50/100/150/200/250 kHz`; deterministic estimates reconstruct
`15.000134/29.999210/45.001603/59.996344/75.003219 m`. For bandwidths
`10/15/20/25/30 MHz` at fixed duration and range, theoretical beats are
`75/112.5/150/187.5/225 kHz`; deterministic estimates reconstruct
`45.002105/44.998247/45.001603/44.997751/44.999598 m`. The broken one-way
formula returns exactly twice the same FFT-derived monostatic estimate, and
restoring the denominator factor two recovers the unchanged baseline value.
These are simulated numerical results, not MATLAB runtime results.

## Residual risks

- MATLAB syntax, local-function behavior, graphics, assertions, numerical
  equivalence, and figure rendering were not runtime-tested.
- The standard-library oracle executes an independent full radix-2 FFT and
  positive-spectrum peak search, but cannot prove MATLAB runtime equivalence.
- The ideal stationary single-target model omits Doppler, multipath, multiple
  targets, leakage, phase noise, ADC effects, propagation loss, and chirp
  nonlinearity.
- The three-bin interpolated peak can estimate an isolated high-SNR tone more
  finely than `c/(2B)`; that does not establish two-target resolution below the
  bandwidth limit.
- The broken case isolates the monostatic propagation factor. It does not test
  a moving target, reversed hardware mixer, aliased beat, or missing-overlap
  runtime path.
- Manual learner observation and teach-back remain required before personal
  completion; pedagogy effectiveness was not mechanically validated.
- Hosted Control plane validation and target Quality CI were inspected but not
  executed for P69 because no push or pull request was authorized.
