## Figure and metric inventory

1. **Motion/path-to-phase:** platform position in metres; line-of-sight error
   in millimetres; injected and centered estimated two-way phase in radians.
2. **Ideal/blurred/corrected images:** cross-range and ground-range offset in
   metres; normalized magnitude in dB; truth crosses; peak retention and mean
   cross-range-cut entropy in titles.
3. **Error-magnitude sweep:** path-error RMS/wavelength; injected phase RMS in
   radians; mean coherent peak/ideal and mean cross-range-cut entropy in nats.
4. **Error-composition sweep:** short-correlated fraction; mean coherent
   peak/ideal; maximum adjacent phase step in radians with the reviewed bound.
5. **Broken/recovery:** normalized dB images in metres plus injected, mixed-gate,
   and isolated-gate centered phase traces in radians.

Metrics are calculated from unclipped complex/linear values. The `-45 dB`
floor is display-only. The script also retains per-gate half-power width in
metres, centered phase RMSE in radians, exact recovery state, and operation and
workspace accounting.
