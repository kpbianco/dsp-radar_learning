## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The independent oracle in `tests/test_p67_module.py` uses Python standard
library complex arithmetic. It reproduces the Park–Miller generator, MATLAB
column-major Box–Muller layout, seeded error patterns, banded coupling,
nominal/physical steering, exact known-source composite calibration, and
physical pattern comparisons without importing or executing `experiment.m`.
Its finite-record checks additionally form the same covariance, use a
partial-pivot complex linear solve to reproduce the loaded MVDR weights,
propagate the diagonal equalizer into the analytical receiver-noise power, and
use block power iteration to reproduce the whitened MUSIC signal-subspace
projection and peak selection.

- Seed 6701 begins with uniforms
  `[0.05244450040741102, 0.4347183473569892, 0.31126402891765537,
  0.4145340190336732]`. Noise seed 6704 produces sample `(1,1)`
  `-1.713299779998622 + j0.11025458408080205` and sample `(10,512)`
  `1.0144430700536382 + j0.6242392797159259` in MATLAB indexing.
- The noiseless physical known-direction response has normalized vector error
  `0.5289445189897005` (`-5.531797574806571 dB`). Exact composite diagonal
  correction reduces it to `8.918570889060244e-17` and restores the nominal
  scan peak at `+10.0 deg`.
- Across the `-40:0.1:40 deg` grid, conventional physical-pattern RMS error
  relative to ideal falls from `0.1223183896888016` to
  `0.04823844964152016` after the direction-specific correction.
- On the finite 512-snapshot record, loaded-MVDR known-source response is
  `-8.5249380943 dB` before calibration and `+0.0019106930 dB` afterward, an
  `8.5268487873 dB` material restoration. This test caught and repaired an
  initially overstrict 10 dB runtime assertion; the retained threshold is 8 dB.
- On that same finite record, calibrated output receiver-noise power is
  `0.07057348856777898`, and output SINR using `E E^H` is
  `20.80705003502867 dB`. Incorrectly retaining white covariance gives
  `19.354574787305697 dB`, a `1.452475247722973 dB` discrepancy in the opposite
  direction from the lesson's former one-sided claim. The permanent behavioral
  test binds both the numerical consequence and the MATLAB data-flow marker.
- For coupling magnitudes `[0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3]`, corrected off-angle normalized
  manifold errors are `[0.13567179969020854, 0.1190634225778333,
  0.13482491022508827, 0.16779157677071965]`. The nonzero, nonmonotonic record
  supports a local-calibration claim, not a universal or monotonic one.
- The wrong broadside-reference equalizer maps the known physical response to
  an all-ones vector and its nominal scan peak to `0.0 deg`; restoring the
  known steering reference recovers `+10.0 deg` on the same vector.
- On the finite noisy pilot and 512-snapshot operational record, loaded Capon
  selects `[-15.2, +9.7] deg` before calibration and `[-14.8, +10.0] deg` after
  correct calibration. Reusing that same data with the wrong broadside
  reference moves the peaks to `[-25.4, 0.0] deg`, increasing matched RMSE from
  about `0.14 deg` after correct calibration to about `10.20 deg`.
- On that unchanged finite record, the standard-library MUSIC oracle selects
  `[-15.2, +9.7] deg` before calibration, `[-14.9, +10.0] deg` after covariance
  and dictionary whitening, and `[-25.5, 0.0] deg` with the wrong broadside
  reference. Treating the calibrated receiver noise as white instead selects
  `[-14.8, +10.0] deg`, so the test makes omission of whitening observable while
  retaining the reviewed calibrated RMSE below `0.1 deg` and broken-case RMSE
  above `10 deg`.

These are simulated numerical results. They are not MATLAB-runtime,
rendered-figure, antenna, bench, hardware/HIL, field, or operational evidence.

## Independent system-risk repair

- **Moderate — calibrated MUSIC behavior lacked an independent executable
  oracle:** the source contract required receiver-noise whitening and the MATLAB
  script asserted calibrated MUSIC recovery, but no available runtime executed
  that path and the retained Python oracle stopped at MVDR/Capon and analytical
  noise power. A new standard-library block-power subspace oracle now uses the
  exact finite pilot and operational record, whitens both the calibrated data
  and nominal dictionary, distinguishes the result from ordinary unwhitened
  MUSIC, and verifies correct recovery plus wrong-reference failure. MATLAB
  source-route markers remain mutation-tested; this does not claim MATLAB
  runtime execution.
- **Moderate — broken-case behavior was not covered on the operational path:**
  the existing oracle proved noiseless phase flattening and a single-vector scan
  shift, but did not form the finite noisy calibration estimate, operational
  covariance, loaded Capon scan, separated peaks, or matched DOA error used by
  the claimed experiment. The new standard-library behavioral oracle reuses the
  exact finite pilot and operational record, reproduces the correct and wrong
  diagonal equalizers, and verifies impaired, recovered, and corrupted Capon
  peaks plus the material RMSE separation.
- **Moderate — missing behavioral noise-covariance coverage:** the MATLAB script
  correctly passed `sigma_n^2 E E^H` into calibrated component metrics, but the
  permanent test suite checked only the formula's source text. A regression
  that computed the covariance but passed white noise into the metrics could
  preserve the old marker while misreporting output SINR. The new finite-record
  behavioral test verifies the calibrated noise power and SINR, compares them
  with the incorrect white-noise result, and binds the calibrated metrics call
  to `calibrated_noise_covariance`.
- **Moderate — calibrated MUSIC initially ignored colored receiver noise:**
  diagonal equalization changes white post-chain noise into `sigma_n^2 E E^H`,
  but ordinary MUSIC assumes a white noise subspace. The repaired script passes
  the correct noise covariance through the baseline, both sweeps, and broken
  path, then applies `R_n^-1/2` to both covariance and nominal scan dictionary
  before the eigenspace split. Source mutation tests bind that route.
- **Moderate — Python behavior and MATLAB route could diverge:** independent
  numerical oracles did not alone prove that `experiment.m` routed the
  physical manifold, desired/interferer powers, pilot manifold, inversion
  guard, compensation, and sweep-specific matrices correctly. Exact route
  markers, minimum occurrence counts, and representative mutation tests now
  reject bypasses, while a sweep oracle confirms each control changes only its
  intended fixed array cause.
- **Moderate — one-sided claim about the resulting error:** `lesson.md` said
  ignoring colored noise would credit calibration with free improvement. In
  the reviewed deterministic record, white-noise substitution instead
  understates calibrated SINR by about 1.45 dB. The lesson and walkthrough now
  state the correct two-sided dependency on equalizer magnitudes and weights.
- **Moderate — accepted controls conflicted with downstream assumptions:** the
  MATLAB validator formerly accepted the interferer as calibration target,
  source angles at scan endpoints on an undersampled grid, and seeds whose
  derived `+1` through `+5` streams were invalid. The validator and independent
  mirror now require the declared desired source, enough strictly interior scan
  samples, and room for every derived stream; negative fixtures cover each case.
- **Moderate — deterministic/resource tests could false-pass source mutants:**
  Python oracles were correct but did not bind the MATLAB seed route, LCG
  recurrence, validator entry, or working-value assertions. The source contract
  and representative mutation test now bind those operations and both preflight
  and post-processing resource checks.
- **Low — coupling and completion wording exceeded the evidence:** the tied
  next-nearest term changes with the one base coupling control, and off-angle
  residuals need not grow monotonically. README, lesson, walkthrough, and checks
  now describe the fixed `0.30*c^2` relation and say residual error remains and
  can grow.
- **Low — moving catalog ranges lacked shared derivation:** the shared
  curriculum test now derives both implemented and pending ranges from the
  manifest, leaving P67's permanent test free of later-module status claims.

## Residual risks and known content gaps

- MATLAB syntax and figure rendering were not runtime-tested. Static source
  contracts and a Python oracle cannot prove MATLAB graphics or platform
  behavior.
- The independent oracle validates the manifold, exact-calibration limits,
  finite-record loaded covariance/MVDR weights, finite-record Capon scan, and a
  standard-library block-power reproduction of whitened MUSIC peaks. It does
  not execute MATLAB's `eig`, prove MATLAB-platform numerical equivalence, or
  inspect rendered spatial-spectrum figures.
- One pilot angle estimates a composite diagonal response, not individual
  positions, gain/phase causes, or a global mutual-coupling inverse.
- The coupling matrix is a bounded illustrative reciprocal banded model, not a
  measured or electromagnetic antenna model.
- The model is narrowband, far-field, stationary, and isotropic. It omits
  broadband coupling, element patterns, polarization, near field, multipath,
  colored external noise, calibration drift, and time-varying channels.
- Receiver noise is post-error-matrix by construction. Other hardware noise
  locations require a different calibration/noise model.
- Manual learner observation and teach-back remain required before personal
  completion; educational effectiveness was not mechanically validated.
- Hosted Control plane and target Quality CI were inspected but not executed
  locally; repository-local commands reproduce target Quality validation.
- The `verify-change` skill requests `contracts/evidence.schema.json` and a
  conforming `evidence.json`, but this repository has no such schema and P67's
  approved scope permits only `docs/evidence/P67-*.md`. This retained Markdown
  artifact is therefore the in-scope evidence format.
