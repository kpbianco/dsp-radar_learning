## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The permanent Python standard-library oracle reproduces the private
Park-Miller/Box-Muller stream, all 3,200 complex chirp samples, the 3,176-sample
valid overlap, explicit delayed/Doppler-shifted echo, `tx * conj(rx)`, and
signed lag-one phase estimate.

For `f_c = 77 GHz`, `fs = 80 MHz`, `T = 40 us`, `B = 20 MHz`,
`S = 0.5 THz/s`, `R = 45 m`, and `v = +20 m/s`:

- delay is `0.300 us` and the target travels `0.800 mm` during the chirp;
- delay contributes `150.000 kHz`, Doppler contributes `10.266667 kHz`, and
  the ideal signed beat is `139.733333 kHz`;
- the seeded noisy phase estimate is `139.738258 kHz`;
- stationary conversion reports `41.921477 m`, bias `-3.078523 m`;
- the deliberately wrong sign reports `38.841477 m`, bias `-6.158523 m`; and
- correct independently known Doppler reports `45.001477 m`, error
  `+0.001477 m`, from the unchanged measured beat.

The exact physical bias law gives `+4.62/+2.31/0/-2.31/-4.62 m` for velocities
`-30/-15/0/+15/+30 m/s`. At `+20 m/s`, bandwidths
`10/15/20/25/30 MHz` give ideal biases
`-6.160/-4.106667/-3.080/-2.464/-2.053333 m`. These are simulated numerical
results, not MATLAB runtime results.
