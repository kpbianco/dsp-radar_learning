## Experiment and metric inventory

   magnitude in dB re 1 V, IRR in dB, DC in dBc.
2. Separate I/Q trajectories: I and Q in volts; clean, DC-only, gain-only,
   phase-only, and combined geometry.
3. Staged correction spectra: raw, mean-removed, gain-normalized, and
   phase-shear-corrected states; IRR in dB and `|DC|` in volts.
4. I-branch gain sweep: dimensionless `gI`, dimensionless ellipse-axis ratio,
   and IRR in dB for `[1, 1.10, 1.30]`.
5. Quadrature error sweep: error in degrees, dimensionless I/Q correlation,
   and IRR in dB for `[0, 5, 15]`.
6. Broken/recovery comparison: I/Q volts plus signed-frequency spectrum,
   contrasting invariant-IRR global rotation with the shear inverse.

Retained workspace outputs include raw/separate/corrected sample vectors,
frequency axis, case DC/IRR/correlation, estimated DC/gains/phase, correction
stage metrics, both sweep metrics, final RMSE in volts, and broken IRR in dB.
