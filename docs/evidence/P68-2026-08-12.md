## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The permanent `test_exact_finite_record_oracle_reproduces_retained_metrics`
reproduces the exact Park-Miller/Box-Muller private streams and reviewed
8-element, 8-pulse finite training record with standard-library Python. With
61 clutter patches, slope `0.35 cycles/pulse per
sin(angle)`, 30 dB total clutter-to-noise power, target at `10.7 deg / 0.208
cycles/pulse`, and the declared small mismatch:

- separate spatial/Doppler loaded-MVDR output SCNR: `-9.125680 dB`;
- joint 64-dimensional loaded-MVDR output SCNR: `+16.462679 dB`;
- clean-prefix support SCNR for `8/16/32/64/128` cells:
  `-6.798879 / +5.923644 / +15.235820 / +15.630305 / +16.462679 dB`;
- 40-percent, 20 dB target-like contamination output SCNR: `-7.395032 dB`;
- clean same-data recovery output SCNR: `+16.462679 dB`.

Permanent tests also use a separate standard-library 6-by-6 analytical oracle
to verify the ridge law and marginal overlap, constrained normalization, joint
improvement over a separable product by more than 20 dB, contamination loss by
more than 15 dB with actual-target attenuation, and deterministic recovery.
The matched-contamination behavior test also captures the implementation's
trace-scaled loading: a 100-power rank-one addition on the exact constrained
vector changes the analytical weight by `0.124130 percent` while preserving
unit constrained response. Its response to the nearby actual target remains
`+0.057988 dB`; contamination on the mismatched actual vector instead reduces
that response to `-3.341364 dB`.
These are simulated numerical results, not MATLAB runtime results.

## Residual risks

- MATLAB syntax, local-function behavior, graphics, assertions, numerical
  equivalence, and figure rendering were not runtime-tested.
- Static route markers and Python oracles cannot prove MATLAB execution.
- The covariance model assumes independent homogeneous training range cells;
  real terrain can be nonstationary and non-Gaussian.
- The ideal ridge omits antenna patterns, crab angle, range dependence,
  internal clutter motion, calibration/coupling error, bandwidth, range
  migration, and platform acceleration.
- The diagonal-loading factor is fixed for teaching, while absolute loading
  follows each covariance trace; it is not an operational selection rule. The
  2x degrees-of-freedom heuristic is illustrative, not a universal
  sample-support guarantee.
- The contaminated case uses a declared small mismatch and synthetic injected
  signature. It is not a measured sidelobe-target or heterogeneous-clutter case.
- Manual learner observation and teach-back remain required before personal
  completion; pedagogy effectiveness was not mechanically validated.
- Hosted Control plane validation and target Quality CI were inspected but not
  executed for P68 because no push/PR was authorized.
