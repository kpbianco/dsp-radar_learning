dimensionless scales.

1. **Bits to symbol coordinates:** BPSK input bits and `I=2b-1`; QPSK I/Q
   input bits; normalized `(+/-1 +/- j)/sqrt(2)` I/Q coordinates versus symbol
   index for the first 24 of 400 symbols.
2. **Baseline constellations and decisions:** received BPSK and QPSK I/Q
   clusters at `Eb/N0=6 dB` and 12-degree phase error; ideal states; fixed
   I=0/Q=0 boundaries; BER; and bit-error flags/counts versus symbol index.
3. **SNR-only sweep:** `Eb/N0=[-4,0,4,8] dB`; fixed zero phase error, bit
   sequences, unit-noise realizations, mapping, and decisions; received QPSK
   clusters plus retained BPSK/QPSK BER vectors.
4. **Carrier-phase-only sweep:** `phi=[0,15,30,50] deg`; fixed `Eb/N0=8 dB`,
   bit sequences, unit-noise realizations, mapping, and decision boundaries;
   received clusters plus retained BPSK/QPSK BER vectors.
5. **Broken reference and recovery:** tight QPSK clusters at `Eb/N0=16 dB`
   with an uncorrected 55-degree rotation; bit errors and BER; exact inverse
   rotation; recovered errors and BER; and negative signed ideal decision
   margin after the boundary crossing.

Retained `results` include controls; generated bits; ideal and received BPSK/
QPSK symbols; detected bits, error counts, BER, and unit-energy checks; both
sweep controls, received QPSK cells, and BPSK/QPSK BER vectors; and the broken
