## Independent system-risk review repair

One medium-severity correctness gap was found and repaired within P14 scope.
The required segment-length lesson assumes the two tones are farther apart than
the longest-segment Hann main-lobe width but closer than the shortest-segment
width. Previously, an edit could violate that physical boundary, perform all
random/FFT work, and only then fail a realization-specific prominence
assertion—or produce a sweep that no longer taught the accepted tradeoff.

`experiment.m` now derives the two boundary widths and rejects incompatible
tone spacing before the private stream or FFT allocation. The retained
`results` structure exposes the tone separation. The independent Python model
now evaluates the canonical noisy tone bins for all three segment lengths and
behaviorally verifies 7/15/31 segments plus more than 5 dB of long-versus-short
weak-tone valley degradation. Negative tests reject equality at both boundary
widths. No contract, CLI, package, external interface, dependency, migration,
or forbidden path was changed by this repair.
