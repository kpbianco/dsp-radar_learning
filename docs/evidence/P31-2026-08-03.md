## Independent oracle results

`tests/test_p31_module.py` independently reconstructs the unit-energy sampled
Gaussian pulse, zero-extended fractional echoes, explicit matched response,
local-max criterion, half-power width, parabolic estimator, and seeded
single-target SNR sweep in Python.

- For 2/4/8 MHz at fixed 22 m separation, measured widths decrease strictly and
  physical peak counts are `[1, 1, 2]`.
- For 10/22/45 m at fixed 4 MHz, physical peak counts are `[1, 1, 2]` and the
  clean response width is unchanged.
- The clean isolated-target refined error is smaller than integer-bin error and
  less than one twentieth of the measured response width.
- Sixteen-times linear display interpolation leaves one physical peak; its two
  largest samples are adjacent and do not represent the true 22 m pair.
- An additional independent seeded 128-trial probe used the sampled clean peak
  `0.995833834999` to realize each SNR and produced:
  `0 dB: bias=-4.412788 m, std=45.285779 m, RMSE=45.323864 m`;
  `15 dB: bias=-0.130994 m, std=2.583411 m, RMSE=2.576632 m`;
  `30 dB: bias=-0.019772 m, std=0.447242 m, RMSE=0.445930 m`.
  This probe uses Python’s deterministic Gaussian generator, not MATLAB’s
  `RandStream`, so it validates the model trend rather than exact MATLAB output.
