## Experiment, figure, and metric inventory

unit amplitude, 12,000 independent records under each hypothesis, baseline
matched-filter SNR of 6 dB, and a real per-sample noise standard deviation
derived as `A*sqrt(E/SNR)`. The normalized score has unit variance under both
hypotheses and mean separation `d' = sqrt(SNR)`. Explicit baseline and
sweep-case assertions reconstruct `A^2 E / sigma^2` and require it to equal
each declared linear SNR, preventing a self-consistent but mislabeled dB scale.

1. **Known-pulse signal flow:** known signal, one H0 record, and one H1 record
   versus within-pulse sample index; empirical H0/H1 normalized statistic
   probability mass versus score in noise-sigma units. Endpoint bins absorb
   both unbounded Gaussian tails and conservation assertions require each
   displayed distribution to sum to one.
2. **Receiver operating characteristic:** empirical and analytic `P_D` versus
   `P_FA`, with the 1.5-noise-sigma operating point marked and the exact
   infinite-threshold `(1,1)`/`(0,0)` limits included.
3. **Threshold consequences:** empirical and analytic `P_FA` and `P_D` versus
   normalized threshold, making their common monotone decrease visible.
4. **Estimator limits:** empirical amplitude bias versus matched-filter SNR in
   dB; empirical variance, CRLB, and `RMSE^2` versus SNR with squared normalized
   amplitude units.
5. **Selection-bias failure and recovery:** true amplitude, all-trial mean, and
   detected-only mean; recovered all-trial bias, empirical selected bias, and
   analytic selected bias in normalized amplitude units.

## Independent review and repair disposition

Two final read-only audits found no defect in the implemented detector
normalization, Gaussian probability model, amplitude estimator, CRLB formula,
truncated-Gaussian selection bias, deterministic replay, resource bounds, or
base-MATLAB operations. They did identify and resolve:

- a medium regression gap: the Python oracle proved independent hypotheses but
  did not bind that claim to the MATLAB source. The permanent test now requires
  the two baseline and two recovery `randn` calls to use their named private
  streams and rejects any extra/unscoped draw;
- a medium pedagogical overgeneralization: the absolute amplitude CRLB falls
  when noise power falls or coherent template energy grows, but not when SNR is
  increased solely by increasing the unknown amplitude. The module now states
  that the plotted SNR sweep changes noise at fixed amplitude/energy and
  distinguishes absolute from relative error;
- two low-severity clarity gaps: the ROC plot now includes exact infinite-
  threshold endpoints, and `d'=sqrt(SNR)` is explicitly limited to the
  experiment's known positive signal polarity.

This independent system-risk review found one additional medium regression
gap: static CI proved the detector and amplitude estimator marginals but did not
bind the detected-only estimate to the same H1 matched-filter records. A source
regression could select with the H0 bank while leaving individually plausible
ROC and estimator distributions. The added behavioral test proves the exact
linear score/estimate relationship, verifies nested thresholds select fewer
records with increasing conditional bias, and binds the MATLAB broken case to
`score_h1` and its corresponding H1 amplitude estimates.
