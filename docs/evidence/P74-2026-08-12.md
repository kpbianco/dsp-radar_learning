## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The permanent Python standard-library tests independently mirror the reviewed
motion law, Park-Miller/Box-Muller private generator, raw phase sign, direct
windowed frequency projections, sweep formulas, magnitude-only failure, and
unchanged-data recovery. The oracle uses no MATLAB output.

For `c = 3e8 m/s`, `f_c = 24 GHz`, `lambda = 0.0125 m`, `fs = 4.8 kHz`, a
four-second dwell, `+1.2 m/s` bulk approach, and `+/-2 m/s` limb swing:

- physical bulk Doppler is `+192 Hz` and the raw dechirped phasor frequency is
  `-192 Hz`;
- limb extent is `+/-320 Hz`, giving physical extrema `-128` and `+512 Hz`;
- a direct one-second Hann projection at raw `-192 Hz` exceeds the incorrect
  `+192 Hz` projection by more than eight times;
- the phase derivative at the first sample matches raw frequencies
  `[-192, -512, +128] Hz` for torso, limb A, and limb B within `0.001 Hz`;
- speed cases `[1, 2, 3] m/s` give bounds `[32, 352]`, `[-128, 512]`, and
  `[-288, 672] Hz` around fixed bulk;
- carrier cases `[10, 24, 77] GHz` give bulk Dopplers
  `[80, 192, 616] Hz` and limb extents
  `[133.333333, 320, 1026.666667] Hz`;
- the worst reviewed `4.2 m/s` component at `77 GHz` is `2156 Hz`, below the
  `2400 Hz` slow-time Nyquist limit; and
- magnitude-only DC projection exceeds its residual bulk-frequency projection
  by more than five times, while the unchanged complex record restores the
  bulk projection and remains exactly equal to its immutable copy.

A separate NumPy development oracle reproduced the MATLAB column order and
explicit STFT geometry to assess assertions that static source inspection
cannot execute. It found:

- deterministic clean-return RMS `1.1061483820` and first unit-variance private
  generator sample `-1.6836367951 - j0.1183578838`;
- baseline, short-window, and long-window frame counts `147`, `397`, and `47`;
- magnitude-only strongest integrated displayed frequency `0 Hz`;
- recovered strongest integrated displayed frequency `192.1875 Hz`; and
- the initial partial logical-payload inventory was `4,125,440`; audit found
  that it omitted live derived arrays, so that value is not retained as a
  resource-bound claim.

These are simulated development-oracle results, not MATLAB runtime or rendered

## Independent audit findings and repairs

The read-only physics audit confirmed the integrated displacement model,
P70-P73 raw sign, positive-approaching display reversal, R2016b syntax
plausibility, private generator order, frame counts, sweep values, and
broken/recovered peaks. It found three resource/isolation gaps that were
repaired before full verification:

- the initial explicit working-value sum omitted live derived arrays; P74 now
  preflights a conservative `12,466,688` eight-byte-equivalent prediction
  against a `15,000,000` ceiling and uses MATLAB `whos` to measure every live
  workspace byte at runtime, counting complex values twice;
- the original sweep reused the same standardized random draw but scaled it by
  case-dependent clean RMS; the model now uses one fixed
  `0.06162450196` nominal additive-noise RMS and returns/asserts the actual
  additive samples, so speed and carrier cases change only their named cause;
- Nyquist preflight originally checked sweep extrema only; it now takes the
  maximum across baseline and sweep swing speeds and carrier frequencies, with
  permanent negative cases for baseline-only aliasing.

The independent delivery audit agreed on the first two gaps and verified that
allowed-path handling, permanent frontier policy, CLI compatibility, and EOF
policy were otherwise sound. It also required replacing provisional evidence
language with the final full-verification and whitespace results. Both audits
were read-only; the principal integration owner made and verified every repair.

The subsequent independent system-risk review found and repaired two additional
medium-risk validation gaps inside the P74 allowlist:

- `window_overlap_fraction` accepted values outside `[0,1)`, so a zero or
  negative sweep hop could survive top-level validation and fail only after
  earlier processing. `validate_controls` now rejects that geometry before
  baseline allocation, with a permanent negative regression case.
- the STFT FFT-length preflight covered `window_sweep_samples` but not an
  independently enlarged baseline window. It now covers the maximum of both,
  with a permanent negative regression case.

The review also found that same-noise physical-sweep isolation was protected by
source markers and MATLAB assertions but lacked an independent behavioral
regression. The new standard-library oracle constructs speed and carrier cases,
proves that their clean returns change, proves that their actual additive-noise
vectors remain exactly equal, and checks that `received - clean` recovers those
samples to floating-point tolerance. The reviewed baseline and physics model
were unchanged.

## Residual risks

- MATLAB was not installed, so parser, numeric, and graphics behavior were
  assessed statically and with independent oracles rather than the named
  runtime.
- Figures were not rendered or manually reviewed; color range, overlay
  visibility, and interactive learning quality remain runtime/manual risks.
- The model starts at one selected range-bin slow-time return; it does not
  repeat FMCW fast-time processing or validate range-Doppler association.
- Three ideal coherent point scatterers are not a full human/rotor model.
  Range migration, aspect RCS, occlusion, multipath, arbitrary acceleration,
  clutter, range leakage, phase noise, antennas, and classification are omitted.
- At coherent nulls and track crossings, composite phase and brightest-ridge
  identity are ambiguous by design.
- The display sign reversal is internally tested, but only actual MATLAB
  rendering can confirm final axis presentation and overlays.
- The 15,000,000 eight-byte-equivalent preflight is deliberately conservative;
  MATLAB's `whos` check measures the complete live workspace at runtime, but
  that final measured value was not executable in this environment.
