## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The permanent Python standard-library oracle reproduces the exact Park-Miller
and Box-Muller private stream, all `512 x 64` complex samples, manual Hann
windows, 512-point fast-time FFTs for every chirp, and 64-point slow-time FFTs
for every retained range row. It searches local two-dimensional neighborhoods
rather than accepting truth coordinates without observing the map.

For `fc = 77 GHz`, `fs = 12.8 MHz`, `T = 40 us`, `B = 150 MHz`,
`S = 3.75 THz/s`, chirp interval `50 us`, 512 samples, and 64 chirps:

- target ranges `20/20/23 m` produce beats `500/500/575 kHz`;
- baseline range spacing is `1.000000 m`;
- baseline velocity spacing is `0.608766 m/s`;
- bin-centered target velocities are `-1.826299/+1.826299/+1.826299 m/s`;
- the measured local maxima occur at `(20.000000 m, -1.826299 m/s)`,
  `(20.000000 m, +1.826299 m/s)`, and
  `(23.000000 m, +1.826299 m/s)` in three distinct cells; and
- after magnitude discards slow-time phase, the isolated `23 m` target's
  strongest Doppler is `0.000000 m/s`.

The ideal sampled phase oracle separately verifies a positive fast-time phase
increment for the beat and a negative slow-time phase increment for the
positive-velocity target to within `2e-14`.

For chirp counts `16/32/64`, CPI durations are `0.8/1.6/3.2 ms` and velocity
spacings are `2.435065/1.217532/0.608766 m/s`. For retained sample counts
`128/256/512`, observed sweep bandwidths are `37.5/75/150 MHz` and range
spacings are `4/2/1 m`. These are simulated numerical results, not MATLAB
runtime results.
