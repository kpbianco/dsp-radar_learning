   with output voltage in volts and time in seconds.

## Independent numerical contracts

`tests/test_p06_module.py` implements separate Python versions of causal
convolution, circular convolution, delay, moving average, echo, and the
second-order resonator recurrence. These are independent host-language checks,
not MATLAB-seeded runtime substitutes.

- A unit impulse produces only the configured delay tap, five equal averaging
  taps summing to one in the independent fixture, a direct plus delayed echo
  tap, and the analytical damped sinusoid
  `b*r^n*sin((n+1)*theta)/sin(theta)` for the resonator.
- An independent 128-sample two-tone input gives direct and causal-convolution
  agreement below `2e-15` for all four systems.
- Two distinct 128-sample inputs, including a localized level change, now
  exercise superposition with unequal signed scale factors for every system.
  A separate nine-sample input shift verifies that each zero-state output
  shifts by the same amount. Both LTI invariants agree below `1e-12` for the
  delay, moving average, echo path, and second-order resonator.
- The independent resonator response has repeated sign changes at the fixed
  nonzero angle, and radius `0.92` retains nontrivial response at lag 64. This
  guards against regressing the canonical resonator into a first-order low-pass.
- Echo-delay conversion independently maps `[8, 32, 64]` samples at
  `1000 samples/s` to `[8, 32, 64] ms`; `-1/log(r)` increases monotonically for
  radii `[0.25, 0.70, 0.92]`.
- Independent circular convolution of a nonzero-ended signal and two-tap echo
  response creates more than `0.05 V` maximum error and nontrivial error energy
  in the first 24 samples; causal linear convolution recovers exactly.
