## Figure and metric inventory

The source defines six figure groups; they were source-reviewed, not rendered:

1. **Baseline time and I/Q direction** — real/Q amplitude in V versus time in
   ms and Q in V versus I in V, with signed phase-step frequency estimates in
   Hz and real-projection RMSE in V.
2. **Signed centered spectra** — signed frequency in Hz and magnitude in dB re
   1 V for the positive complex tone, negative complex tone, and real
   projection, including peak frequencies and complex-coefficient conjugate-
   symmetry error.
3. **Real versus complex downconversion** — upper/lower complex baseband Q in V
   versus I in V, and paired real baseband amplitude in V versus time in ms;
   metrics report signed complex beats in Hz and real-output RMSE in V.
4. **Frequency-offset sweep** — 40/160/400 Hz paired I/Q trajectories, signed
   estimates in Hz, and real-projection RMSE in V.
5. **Sample-rate sweep** — 2048/512/256 samples/s signed spectra with expected
   and estimated `+160/-160`, `+160/-160`, and `-96/+96 Hz` aliases.
6. **Broken Q discard and recovery** — coincident I-only paths with zero sign
   estimates and recovered paired I/Q trajectories at `+160/-160 Hz`.

The retained `results` structure includes seed/rate/count/time, tone controls,
paired complex and real signals, signed spectra, phase-step frequencies,
projection/symmetry metrics, LO and FIR, complex and real downconversion
outputs/metrics, both sweep controls/results, and broken/recovered estimates.

## Independent audit and repair

The independent system-risk review found one material metric/test defect: the
source labeled a mirrored-magnitude comparison as conjugate-symmetry error.
Equal magnitudes cannot detect a phase-conjugacy violation, so the metric could
falsely report zero for a phase-broken spectrum. The script now compares the
normalized complex FFT coefficients against their conjugate-mirrored partners,
and a behavioral regression proves that a magnitude-symmetric but
phase-nonconjugate fixture fails the repaired metric. No P17 sign/amplitude,
canonical identity, forbidden-path, dependency, licensing, persisted-state, or
external-resource defect was found.

The review also confirmed the cancellation boundary: Ctrl+C is bounded and
causes no external/persisted side effect, but a partial P18 figure set and
empty/incomplete `results` can remain until a full rerun.

## Deterministic CI repair

GitHub Quality run `30761741538` failed on Ubuntu with Python 3.12.13 at the
first and only failing assertion:
`test_offset_sweep_changes_only_rotation_speed_and_retains_collapse` for the
400 Hz case. The independently generated estimate was
`399.9999999999909 Hz`, differing from 400 Hz by
`9.094947017729282e-12 Hz`; all preceding checks and the other P18 checks
passed. The failure was therefore in the cross-platform numerical fixture,
not in the MATLAB experiment or signed-frequency model.

The fixture formerly evaluated a fresh complex exponential at every sample,
so platform-dependent trigonometric reduction operated on phases growing to
thousands of radians. It now creates the same deterministic complex tone from

production source, and all acceptance thresholds remain unchanged.
