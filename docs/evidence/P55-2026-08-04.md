## Independent system-risk review and repairs

The review found two material medium-severity gaps. First, the canonical
completion condition requires post-warm-up state errors and innovations to
remain broadly consistent with predicted uncertainty, but the MATLAB
reviewed-run assertion covered position and innovation only. The independent
end-to-end oracle also omitted the central baseline consistency behavior, so a
velocity-covariance or reported-completion regression could pass while Q/R
direction checks remained green.

The bounded repair now requires position, velocity, and innovation coverage in
the MATLAB reviewed-run invariant. A new independent behavioral regression
verifies that the baseline smooths position reports, all three two-sigma
coverage fractions exceed the documented majority threshold, posterior
variances remain finite and nonnegative, and innovation variances remain finite
and positive. A mutation binding rejects removal of the MATLAB velocity check.
No contract, infrastructure, unrelated module, runtime claim, or resource
ceiling was changed.

The review also found that the distinct under-Q failure reported position
coverage and velocity RMSE but omitted velocity coverage. In the independent
scene, position coverage can remain unchanged while underestimated Q narrows
the velocity covariance enough to reduce velocity coverage, so the original
outputs and tests could miss the intended overconfidence mechanism. The bounded
repair retains under-Q velocity coverage in the MATLAB workspace/console and
adds an end-to-end behavioral test requiring it to fall below the matched
baseline on the fixed oracle scene. No MATLAB numerical result is claimed.
