the governing equations without parsing MATLAB. Its reduced 32-sample LFM
case produces a unit correct-replica correlation peak at the inserted delay;
the nonconjugated replica peak is lower. A reduced local-power map proves that
every strict-`Pfa` crossing remains present after `Pfa` is relaxed, while a
quiet-side global threshold overspends crossings on a 16-times-power edge.

and the reduced alpha-beta sequence coasts exactly on its missing report and
reacquires afterward. An adversarial scoring fixture has one ambiguous report

`Pd` is maximum-cardinality one-to-one matched reports divided by truth
opportunities, so its value is not truth-list-order dependent. Empirical
false-cell rate excludes guard neighborhoods around truth but does not claim
the homogeneous design Pfa. False reports are unmatched clusters. Range RMSE
uses truth only after tracking. Nominal resolution is `c/(2B)`; range sample
spacing is `c/(2fs)`; velocity-bin spacing is `lambda*PRF/(2Np)`. Runtime is a
measured wall-clock duration only when MATLAB actually runs.
