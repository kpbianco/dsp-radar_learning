## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The independent oracle in `tests/test_p64_module.py` uses Python standard
library complex arithmetic. It reproduces the Park-Miller generator and MATLAB
column-major noise layout without importing or executing `experiment.m`.

- Calibration: 401 plotted angles and 161 calibration points from `-4` through
  `+4 deg`; the ratio is strictly increasing from
  `-0.5020664573616728` to `+0.5020664573616728`, odd about boresight,
  with matched left/right magnitude symmetry and a safe sum response.
- Noise-free inversion: `[-4, -2, 0, 2, 4] deg` are recovered to numerical
  tolerance; ratios outside the local endpoints saturate explicitly at the
  reviewed sector bounds.
- Common-voltage cancellation: four simultaneous noiseless `+2 deg` target
  voltages spanning `0.5` through `5.0` in magnitude and four phases produce
  the same calibrated ratio and recover `+2 deg` to numerical tolerance.
- Seed fixture: seed 6401 begins with uniforms
  `[0.0500965898624140, 0.9733819046399472, 0.6296673706405178,
  0.8194944422317177]`; four indexed complex-noise values bind the MATLAB
  reshape convention.
- Baseline at true `+2 deg`: first-snapshot ratio
  `0.2354045503547323`, coherently averaged ratio
  `0.2341409192657401`, coherent angle estimate
  `1.9928646603789308 deg`, and valid-snapshot RMSE
  `0.1839513638757116 deg`.
- SNR sweep at `[-5, 5, 15, 25] dB`: valid-snapshot RMSE is
  `[1.8149541476915518, 0.6626636698475726, 0.2107300051662726,
  0.0669307394362874] deg`; unsafe-sum rejection counts are `[2, 0, 0, 0]` of
  256; coherent estimates approach the unchanged `+2 deg` truth.
- Squint sweep: local ratio slope increases while normalized boresight sum
  voltage decreases for `[1.5, 3.0, 5.0] deg` squint.
- Broken case: gain `g=1.12` produces exact boresight ratio
  `(g-1)/(g+1)=0.0566037735849057` and false estimate
  `0.4908913734993808 deg`. Four changing common target voltages retain that
  same bias after coherent channel averaging; division by the same gain
  restores ratio and angle to zero on the unchanged right-channel data.

These are simulated numerical results. They are not MATLAB-runtime,
rendered-figure, antenna, bench, field, or operational evidence.
