## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The permanent Python standard-library tests independently construct the
reviewed LFM, private noise, integer-delayed echoes, linear matched responses,
range-axis correction, width interpolation, spacing rule, two-way aperture
phase, and magnitude-only failure. They do not consume MATLAB output.

For `c=3e8 m/s`, `Fs=120 MHz`, `fc=5 GHz`, a 2 microsecond pulse, 20 MHz
baseline bandwidth, an 80 m aperture sampled every 0.2 m, and a 950–1400 m
range gate:

- the wavelength is 0.06 m, the range grid is 1.25 m, the pulse has 240
  samples, `B*T=40`, and the nominal baseline resolution is 7.5 m;
- the raw matrix is `401 x 361`, and full row-wise convolution produces a
  `401 x 600` complex range-compressed phase history;
- an isolated unit echo starting at zero-based delay 40 peaks at output 279,
  and removal of the 239-sample filter delay maps it exactly to 1000 m with
  unit normalized peak;
- 10, 20, and 40 MHz full -3 dB widths are `13.1539937894`,
  `6.55251475046`, and `3.22304308451 m`, strictly decreasing; nominal
  `c/(2B)` scales are 15, 7.5, and 3.75 m;
- 3.75, 10, and 15 m equal-target spacings give valley/weaker-peak ratios
  `0.922431322208`, `0.957646520196`, and `0.0512840247297`; only the last is
  below the reviewed `1/sqrt(2)` resolution rule;
- in the clean three-target baseline, corrected ridge-delay bins at left,
  center, and right aperture positions are respectively `[40, 61, 97]`,
  `[40, 60, 96]`, and `[41, 61, 96]`; every local composite-response peak is
  at its quantized slant-range bin and the largest true-range error is below
  the half-sample `0.625 m` bound;
- noisy complex-ridge phase coherences for the three baseline targets are
  `0.999852232609`, `0.999673454983`, and `0.999508671079`; and
- their magnitude-only expected-phase coherences are approximately 0.07 and
  remain below 0.20 while `abs(complex_data)` is exactly unchanged.

Negative cases reject missing, empty, and placeholder artifacts; nested or
nonfinite vectors; unequal target-property lengths; invalid seeds/counts;

- MATLAB was not installed, so parser, local-function, numeric, assertion,
  runtime-duration, memory-inventory, and graphics behavior were assessed
  statically and with independent oracles rather than in the named runtime.
- Six figures were not rendered or manually reviewed; color contrast, ridge
  visibility, legend placement, and lesson effectiveness remain runtime/manual
  risks.
- The explicit nested row-wise matched filter is intentionally transparent and
  bounded but has not been runtime benchmarked or interrupted with Ctrl+C.
- Integer delay omits fractional-delay phase and limits range placement to half
  a 1.25 m sample. The bandwidth sweep's interpolated width is more precise
  than target-delay placement, but does not claim target range accuracy.
- The rectangular LFM has sidelobes; the deterministic spacing result is bound
  to equal-amplitude, equal-phase targets and is not a universal resolution
  guarantee.
- The model assumes stop-and-go collection, stationary isotropic point targets,
  exact straight-track positions, narrowband carrier phase, and no within-pulse
  Doppler, clutter, multipath, propagation loss, antenna pattern, oscillator
  drift, calibration error, or motion error.
- Range compression preserves rather than corrects the modest range histories;
  P77 owns backprojection, P78 migration correction, P79 SAR resolution and
  windowing, and P80 motion-error autofocus.
