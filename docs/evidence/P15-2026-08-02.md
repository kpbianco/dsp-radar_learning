## Independent numerical and negative contracts

call MATLAB:

  window-normalized power to 12 decimal places, with interior bins doubled and
  DC/Nyquist unchanged;
- an independent 90 Hz full-record tone is measured in early, middle, and late
  baseline frames; all three select the same 88 Hz nearest FFT bin, within the
  8 Hz baseline spacing, covering the promised horizontal steady track;
- an independent gated 220-to-320 Hz linear-chirp model uses the baseline
  128-sample Hann frames and center timestamps; its ridge advances monotonically
  by more than 80 Hz and its mean absolute error remains below the 8 Hz bin
  spacing;
- 512/128/64-sample Hann windows independently yield 2/8/16 Hz grids,
  approximate 8/32/64 Hz null-to-null widths, and 15/63/127 frames;
- the canonical 18 Hz hop spacing is above the long-window width and below the
  short-window width, crossing the intended visibility boundary;
- an independent continuous-phase hop model produces distinct 156 and 174 Hz
  local maxima in the hop-centered 512-sample Hann frame, while the 64-sample
  frame produces one blended local maximum at 160 Hz;
- 0/50/75% overlap at 128 samples independently gives 32/63/125 frames and
  125/62.5/31.25 ms steps without changing window response;
- an independent 64-sample burst model shows that 75% overlap centers the event,
  reduces its 31.25 ms timing error to zero on the canonical grid, and raises the
  nearest-bin peak by more than 5 dB relative to 0% overlap;
- padding 64 samples to 512 DFT points produces 257 one-sided pixels and 2 Hz
  spacing while the physical Hann width remains approximately 64 Hz.
