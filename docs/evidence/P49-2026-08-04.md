## Physical model and independent static oracle

The experiment uses 24 independent exponential square-law training powers,
two guard cells per side, and a requested homogeneous `Pfa` of `1e-3`. Four
20 dB noncoherent point-power additions occupy the 15 dB primary target's
reference window. Sweep CUTs use a deterministic complex amplitude in complex
Gaussian noise before square-law detection. CA multiplies the arithmetic mean
by its 24-cell factor. OS sorts ascending,
selects rank 18, and multiplies that one power by its rank-specific factor.
Thus six sufficiently high samples can remain above the selected rank; this is
a capacity bound, not a claim that contamination leaves the statistic
unchanged.

For `N = 24` and homogeneous `Pfa = 1e-3`, an independent Python
implementation of the exact product probability and fixed bisection gives:

- CA scale: `8.004514371919775`;
- rank-18 OS scale: `6.502430709645976`;
- recovered rank-12 OS scale: `13.996715751543068`;
- calibrated rank-18 homogeneous `Pfa`: approximately `0.001`; and
- broken rank-18 scale reused at rank 12: `Pfa = 0.024385022411216258`.

A separate 20,000-trial seeded Python oracle, which does not reproduce
MATLAB's sample stream, measured paired `(CA, OS-rank-18)` detection
probabilities of approximately `(0.000, 0.841)` with four 20 dB contaminants,
`(0.000, 0.452)` with six, and `(0.000, 0.000)` with seven or eight. With four
contaminators, raising each from `-20` to `20` dB changed paired detection from
approximately `(0.988, 0.977)` to `(0.000, 0.841)`; the OS result remained
approximately `0.841` at 30 dB because the four powers already occupied the
highest ranks. With four 20 dB contaminants and rank-specific calibration,
rank-12, rank-18, and rank-22 detection probabilities were approximately
`0.886`, `0.841`, and `0.000`. These checks support the outlier-capacity,
strength-plateau, rank-masking, and calibration claims. The independent review
added the missing clean-scene comparison: rank-12 and rank-18 detection were
approximately `0.963` and `0.977`, respectively. The preference therefore
reverses in this bounded model: rank 18 is better in homogeneous references,
while rank 12 is better with four strong reference outliers. This demonstrates
the documented detection cost of buying more outlier capacity instead of
presenting robustness as free. These checks are simulated mathematical
evidence, not MATLAB execution, sample-for-sample MATLAB results, rare-event
validation, or radar-system evidence.
