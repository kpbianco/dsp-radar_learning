## Experiment and figure/metric inventory

All axes and metrics identify units or explicitly state dimensionless ranges.

1. **Noisy rotating phasor:** time in milliseconds, I and Q in volts, and a
   complex-plane I/Q trajectory in volts for the clean/noisy 48-sample view.
2. **Baseline frequency estimates:** FFT magnitude in dB re 1 V versus hertz;
   truth, peak-bin, and interpolated locations; frequency errors in hertz; and
   dimensionless adjacent-product coherence.
3. **Coherent phase estimates:** residual phase in radians versus milliseconds
   after each candidate frequency is removed; wrapped initial-phase errors in
   radians.
4. **SNR sweep:** one fixed standardized-noise bank scaled by SNR in dB;
   frequency bias and standard deviation in hertz;
   circular phase bias and standard deviation in degrees; mean coherence across
   40 deterministic trials per case on its own dimensionless 0–1 axis.
5. **Record-length sweep:** prefixes of the same standardized-noise trials;
   observation duration in seconds; frequency bias and
   standard deviation in hertz; circular phase bias and standard deviation in
   degrees; fixed physical tone, sample rate, per-sample SNR, and trial count.
6. **Broken/recovery view:** wrapped phase in radians versus milliseconds;
   broken/recovered frequency in hertz; low-amplitude I/Q in volts; baseline and
   low-amplitude coherence versus the dimensionless `0.20` reporting gate.

Retained `results` include seed, sampling controls, duration, tone truth, SNR,
time/IQ/FFT vectors, three baseline frequency/phase estimates and errors,
coherence, SNR-sweep bias/spread/coherence, duration-sweep bias/spread/coherence,
