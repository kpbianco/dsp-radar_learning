## Experiment and figure/metric inventory

All axes and metrics identify units or explicitly state normalized,
dimensionless scales.

1. **Channel impulse and fading:** path magnitude versus delay in symbol
   periods; baseline and broken channel magnitude in dB versus normalized
   frequency `f/R_s` in cycles/symbol.
2. **Waveform and eye closure:** transmitted and multipath I complex-envelope
   amplitude versus symbol periods; 32 direct-path and multipath two-symbol
   matched-filter I traces versus time relative to the decision in symbol
   periods.
3. **Baseline constellations:** unequalized, 31-tap causal ZF, and MMSE I/Q in
   normalized units, with EVM percent in each title and printed SER/noise gain.
4. **Echo-gain sweep:** unequalized/MMSE EVM percent and minimum channel
   magnitude in dB versus the one-symbol echo magnitude.
5. **Deep-null failure/recovery:** causal-ZF and regularized-MMSE constellations;
   EVM/residual response percent and equalizer noise gain in dB versus
   dimensionless `lambda`.

The baseline channel has symbol delays `[0,1,2]` and complex gains
`[1, 0.45 exp(j0.45), 0.20 exp(-j0.80)]`. The broken channel
`[1,-0.999]` has a discrete DC minimum of `-60 dB`. Complex waveform noise is
formed with independent real/imaginary Gaussian components divided by
`sqrt(2)` and scaled by `sqrt(10^(-18/10))`; the unit-energy matched filter
therefore preserves the declared decision-sample noise variance in the ideal
Nyquist model.

The independent standard-library Python oracle—not MATLAB execution—found:

- baseline causal-ZF tap energy `1.3703252717`, exact cancellation of its first
  30 ideal symbol-spaced postcursors, and final residual magnitude below
  `2e-6`;
- broken causal-ZF final residual magnitude `0.999^31`, tap energy
  `30.0881783717`, and a `-60 dB` channel minimum;
- broken `lambda=0.01` regularized tap energy `4.4349354185` and squared
  combined-response residual `0.0502530899`, demonstrating lower noise gain
  than causal ZF while retaining controlled ISI;
- an end-to-end finite-RRC behavioral model with deterministic QPSK and
  deterministic complex Gaussian-like noise produced baseline EVM values of
  `51.580424%` unequalized, `15.137527%` ZF, and `14.782324%` regularized, then
  produced `116.968286%` for broken-channel ZF versus `31.771376%` for the
  `lambda=0.01` recovery. This independently exercises pulse shaping, delayed
  channel convolution, matched-filter sampling, and equalization, but does not
  reproduce MATLAB's private random stream.

Retained `results` include controls; bits/symbols/RRC pulse/transmitted
waveform; baseline delays, gains, impulse/response, samples, equalizers,
EVM/SER; echo-sweep response/EVM; and broken-channel response, ZF and
