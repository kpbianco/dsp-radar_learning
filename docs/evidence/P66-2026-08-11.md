## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The independent oracle in `tests/test_p66_module.py` uses Python standard
library complex arithmetic. It reproduces the Park–Miller generator, MATLAB
column-major Box–Muller noise layout, covariance, deterministic orthogonal
subspace iteration, Bartlett scan, MUSIC projector, local-maximum selection,
and spatial smoothing without importing or executing `experiment.m`.

- Seed fixture: seed 6601 begins with uniforms
  `[0.0516618634814685, 0.280939533040365, 0.750731809414333,
  0.549520826688745]`; four indexed complex-noise values bind the MATLAB
  reshape convention.
- Baseline MUSIC peaks are exactly on the reviewed scan samples at `-3.0 deg`
  and `+3.0 deg`, giving `0 deg` angle RMSE. Bartlett truth-to-midpoint contrast
  is `-0.6523798064 dB`, so its midpoint remains above both truth angles. MUSIC
  contrast is `18.3602312501 dB`, visibly splitting the same pair.
- For source separations `[2,3,4,5,6,8,10,12] deg`, MUSIC angle RMSE is
  `[0.22361,0.14142,0.07071,0.07071,0,0,0,0] deg`. At `4 deg`, Bartlett
  contrast remains `-0.3696354394 dB` while MUSIC contrast is
  `10.9037396633 dB`; Bartlett first becomes positive at the reviewed `12 deg`
  endpoint.
- SNR endpoint behavior on unchanged geometry is intentionally non-universal:
  at `-10 dB`, selected peaks `[-22.3,1.8] deg` give `13.6735 deg` RMSE; at
  `15 dB`, peaks `[-3,3] deg` give `0 deg` RMSE.
- Shared-prefix snapshot behavior at `0 dB` gives peaks `[-23.2,0.7] deg` and
  `14.3758 deg` RMSE with 16 snapshots, versus `[-2.9,2.8] deg` and
  `0.1581 deg` RMSE with 512 snapshots. No monotonic point-by-point claim is
  made.
- On the unchanged baseline covariance, overestimating the source count as
  `K=3` selects `[-19.2,-3,3] deg`; `K=4` selects
  `[-38.8,-21.3,-3,3] deg`. Both retain the two truth peaks while admitting
  deterministic noise-subspace artifacts.
- The coherent broken case selects `[0,14.4] deg`, giving `8.3355 deg` RMSE.
  Averaging four overlapping seven-element subarray covariances from that same
  record recovers `[-3,3.1] deg`, giving `0.07071 deg` RMSE.

These are simulated numerical results. They are not MATLAB-runtime,
rendered-figure, antenna, bench, field, or operational evidence.

## Residual risks and known content gaps

- MATLAB syntax and figure rendering were not runtime-tested in this
  environment. Static source contracts and an independent Python oracle cannot
  prove MATLAB graphics or platform-specific execution.
- The oracle uses deterministic orthogonal subspace iteration rather than
  reproducing MATLAB's exact eigensolver. It validates invariant subspace and
  spectrum behavior, not eigenvector phases or basis choices.
- The model is narrowband, far-field, stationary, and ideal except for finite
  covariance evidence, white receiver noise, and one exact coherence failure.
- Real element patterns, calibration error, mutual coupling, spatially colored
  noise, multipath, near-field curvature, broadband effects, model-order
  estimation, detection, and tracking are not modeled.
- Spatial smoothing relies on a shift-invariant calibrated ULA and reduces the
  effective aperture. Its reviewed recovery is not universal.
- Manual learner observation and teach-back remain required before personal
  completion; educational effectiveness was not mechanically validated.
- Hosted required CI was inspected but not executed locally; repository-local
  commands reproduce the target Quality validation contract.
- The `verify-change` skill requests `contracts/evidence.schema.json` and a
  conforming `evidence.json`, but this repository has no such schema and P66's
  approved paths permit only `docs/evidence/P66-*.md`. This retained Markdown
  record is therefore the in-scope evidence format.
