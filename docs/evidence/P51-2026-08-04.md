## Physical model and independent static oracle

The experiment uses 24 square-law training powers, 12 on each side, three
guards per side, ascending OS rank 18, and requested homogeneous `Pfa=1e-3`.
Its 256-cell power-domain scene combines a 12 dB clutter step, a smooth
long-range noise-power swell, an explicit symmetric compressed-target response,
a weak neighbor, a low-side edge target, and a crowded target group. Target
response power is added noncoherently to seeded independent exponential
background power. That is a transparent teaching model, not raw radar data.

Independent Python evaluation of the exact homogeneous equations gives:

```text
alpha_CA = 8.004514371919775
alpha_GO = 7.089038451813456
alpha_SO = 10.480855745946773
alpha_OS(rank 18) = 6.502430709645976
```

Each statistic-specific value returns `Pfa` approximately `0.001` within
`1e-14`. Reusing CA alpha gives exact homogeneous probabilities
`[0.0010000000, 0.0004729227, 0.0038688671, 0.0002844644]` for
`[CA, GO, SO, OS]`, proving the shared-alpha comparison is not equal-Pfa.

Two hand-built deterministic reference windows establish causal disagreements
without relying on MATLAB sample identity. With CUT power 50, twelve leading
references at power 1, and twelve lagging references at power 10, decisions
are `[CA,GO,SO,OS]=[1,0,1,0]`: GO follows the high half, SO the low half, CA
blends both, and rank 18 occupies the high group. With CUT power 20, four
leading references at power 100 and all other references at power 1, decisions
are `[0,0,1,1]`: one-sided target contamination masks CA/GO while SO and OS
retain the CUT.

A separate four-case clutter-edge oracle holds detector calibration, training
geometry, and normalized CUT powers fixed while the high/low reference contrast
changes through 0, 6, 12, and 18 dB. All four detectors retain the low-side
target through 6 dB. At 12 and 18 dB the decisions become `[0,0,1,0]`: SO
retains that target while CA, GO, and rank-18 OS mask it. For a high-side H0 CUT
held at twice its local mean, the corresponding decisions change from
`[0,0,0,0]` to `[0,0,1,0]`, exposing the false-crossing cost of SO's low-side
choice. This is independent model evidence for Sweep 1, not a MATLAB result.

The OS capacity oracle gives a rank-18 detection with six high outliers and a
miss with seven. A separate 10,000-trial paired Python model measured
CA/GO/SO/OS weak-CUT detection probabilities of approximately
`[0.9871,0.9834,0.9742,0.9762]` with no contaminants,
`[0,0,0,0.4470]` with six 20 dB contaminants, and `[0,0,0,0]` with seven.
These results support the finite `N-k=6` boundary; they do not prove MATLAB
sample-for-sample output or achieved rare-event Pfa. A separate profile oracle
proves full-stencil no-decision borders and exact uniform-power scale
invariance for all four masks.
