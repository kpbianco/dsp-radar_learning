## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The independent Python oracle uses only the standard library and separately
implements the private Park-Miller and Box-Muller records, two-source ULA data
model, conjugate scan weights, direct and covariance power paths, local source
peak measurement, the four one-variable sweeps, sign-error mirror, recovery,
and malformed-input rejection.

- The seed-6301 uniform record begins
  `(0.049313952936471415, 0.819603090323323, 0.06913515113719514, 0.9544812498867891)`.
- Exact complex-noise and received-scene samples bind the independent oracle to
  the MATLAB column-major reshape, Park-Miller sequence, Box-Muller pairing,
  steering sign, noise scaling, and source/noise sum.
- With `M=8`, `d/lambda=0.5`, `L=128`, source angles `[-20,+25] deg`, and
  nominal `+10 dB` per-source/per-sensor SNR, scan peaks occur at
  `[-19.9,+25.0] deg` on the `0.1 deg` grid.
- Direct mean output power and the independently formed `w^H Rhat w` scan
  agree to less than `1e-12` over all 1,201 scan directions.
- For an isolated `-20 deg` arrival, matched normalized contributions sum to
  exactly one to floating-point precision; broadside-mismatched contributions
  sum below `0.25`.
- Separation `6 deg` merges to one measured maximum, while separation `24 deg`
  produces peaks more than `20 deg` apart. For the fixed `-8/+8 deg` pair, four
  elements merge inward while eight and sixteen produce distinct peaks more
  than `16 deg` apart. A negative one-lobe shoulder fixture proves that two
  disjoint truth windows are not called resolved unless both selected samples
  are actual local maxima.
- Relative off-source scan floors decrease monotonically as nominal input SNR
  increases from `-15` through `0` to `+15 dB`. On one unchanged `0 dB`
  record, the 128-look off-source dB ripple is smaller than the one-look ripple.
- Repeating one noiseless `+10 deg` plane-wave snapshot 128 times preserves
  every absolute scan-power sample to within `1e-12`, the `+10 deg` peak, and
  the complete half-power support. Snapshot averaging therefore does not alter
  the fixed array response for unchanged observations.
- For sources at `-20/+30 deg`, wrong-sign peaks are `-29.9/+20.1 deg` and the
  recovered peaks are `-20.1/+29.9 deg`. Every broken scan value equals the
  reversed recovered value to less than `1e-12`.

These are deterministic simulated-oracle results, not MATLAB runtime,
rendered-figure, physical-array, or operational-radar results.
