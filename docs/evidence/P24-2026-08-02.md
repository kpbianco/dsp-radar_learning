## Experiment and figure/metric inventory

All axes and metrics identify units or explicitly state normalized,
dimensionless scales.

1. **Pulse shapes and transmitted waveform:** unit-energy rectangular and RRC
   taps versus symbol periods; I/Q complex-envelope amplitude versus symbol
   periods for the same first 12 of 320 seeded QPSK symbols.
2. **Spectrum:** rectangular/RRC pulse power responses and realized transmitted
   waveform spectra versus normalized frequency `f/R_s`, in dB. The pulse
   comparison reports a documented discrete central 99%-power bandwidth
   estimate in units of `R_s` using a fixed 4096-point FFT.
3. **Matched output and eye:** sample-rate matched-filter I output and retained
   decision samples versus symbol periods; 40 two-symbol I-eye traces centered
   on the compensated symbol clock.
4. **Constellations:** normalized I/Q before the receive matched filter, after
   matched filtering and correct timing, at a noiseless `+0.5T` broken timing
   offset, and after exact timing recovery. Metrics include EVM percent, symbol
   errors, and SER.
5. **Parameter/failure summary:** roll-off versus 99%-power bandwidth divided
   by `R_s`; finite RRC span versus ISI-only EVM percent; correct versus
   half-symbol timing EVM percent.

The rectangular and RRC pulses have unit energy. The RRC receive filter is
`conj(fliplr(rrc_pulse))`; its span-8 transmit/receive cascade delay is 64
samples. `Es/N0=14 dB` baseline noise uses one private complex unit-noise record
and `sqrt(10^(-Es/N0/10))`, so the unit-energy matched-filter output has the
declared noise variance. The noise-free parallel chain separates finite-span
ISI and timing error from noisy total EVM.

Retained `results` include controls; bits/symbols/upsampled impulses; both
pulses, matched filters, and combined responses; transmit/receive/matched
waveforms; spectra and 99%-bandwidth estimates; sample indices and pre/post
samples; EVM/SER; roll-off bandwidth/ISI results; span tap-count/ISI results;
and broken/recovered samples, errors, SER, and EVM. Fixed ceilings are 320
symbols, eight samples/symbol, span eight, four cases per sweep, five figure
groups, and a conservative 500000 stored-numeric-value budget.
