## Residual risks

- MATLAB parsing, execution, figures, assertions, global-RNG isolation,
  repeatability, storage bound, and measured runtime remain unvalidated.
- The independent Python oracle cannot prove identical MATLAB indexing,
  floating-point, graphics, or workspace behavior.
- The reference is only pulse-shaped QPSK-like synthetic baseband, not a real
  broadcast format or recording.
- The one-tap projection chiefly removes zero-delay predictable leakage here,
  but any delayed component correlated with the unshifted reference can bias
  the fitted coefficient or be partially attenuated. Real passive radar
  requires synchronization and extensive direct/multipath cancellation without
  erasing slow targets.
- The narrowband Doppler phasor omits wideband scale, acceleration, oscillator
  and clock offsets, propagation loss, antenna geometry, clutter distributions,
  detection thresholds, and calibration.
- The deterministic endpoint assertions may reveal MATLAB-specific numerical
  differences when a runtime becomes available; they were tuned against the
  independent equations, not hidden with broad tolerances.
- Ctrl+C was not forced, peak memory was statically bounded but not measured,
  human educational effectiveness was not playtested, and the unpushed branch
  has no CI run.
