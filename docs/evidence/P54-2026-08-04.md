## Static behavioral evidence

The retained tracker model is

```text
x_pred(k) = x_hat(k-1) + T v_hat(k-1)
v_pred(k) = v_hat(k-1)
r(k) = z(k) - x_pred(k)
x_hat(k) = x_pred(k) + alpha r(k)
v_hat(k) = v_pred(k) + (beta/T) r(k).
```

When a report is unavailable, corrected position and velocity equal their
predictions and innovation remains unavailable. No zero report is fabricated.
The gains must satisfy `0 < alpha < 2` and `0 < beta < 4-2*alpha`; beta zero is
accepted only by the deliberately broken limiting case.

The independent Python oracle uses handcrafted finite reports rather than
claiming MATLAB random-stream identity. It verifies prediction units, residual
sign, alpha and `beta/T` corrections, zero-innovation identity, alpha-one
measurement following, beta-zero velocity failure, position translation
invariance, prediction-only dropout coasting, post-gap correction, invalid
input rejection, and deterministic repeatability.

Its bounded end-to-end scene independently uses 81 scans, the same 20-to-32 m/s
piecewise-constant truth, two three-scan gaps, and a deterministic nonrandom
error pattern. The baseline reduces steady-interval position RMSE from
15.34 m for available reports to 4.16 m, exhibits a nonzero position lag after
the model-violating velocity step, and eventually exceeds the 26 m/s midpoint.
Across alpha `[0.10 0.35 0.85]`, corrected position moves progressively closer
to the received reports; alpha 0.35 has lower steady truth RMSE than either
endpoint, while alpha 0.10 has greater peak maneuver error than alpha 0.35. On
that independent scene beta 0.30 crosses the midpoint before beta 0.01, while
beta zero leaves all velocity estimates at zero and has larger position RMSE
than the recovered baseline. These are host-language oracle results, not MATLAB
default-run measurements.

## Residual risks

- Static/source and independent Python checks cannot prove MATLAB syntax,
  random-stream sample identity, figure rendering, runtime, memory, or exact
  default numerical outputs.
- Script-local functions require MATLAB R2016b or later. MATLAB-specific
  `RandStream`, `groot`, and plotting calls mean Octave compatibility remains
  unverified.
- The synthetic scalar scene assumes one correct report per available scan. It
  does not model clutter, false reports, association ambiguity, multidimensional
  motion, acceleration, turns, or measured radar errors.
- Fixed alpha and beta do not carry covariance or adapt trust from process and
  measurement noise. The tracker cannot quantify uncertainty growth during a
  dropout.
- A velocity change violates the carried constant-velocity state and creates
  expected lag; a longer dropout or larger initial velocity error can make
  prediction diverge substantially.
- The documented gain stability region does not choose optimal gains for a
  mission or prove operational tracking performance.
