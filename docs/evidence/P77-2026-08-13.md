## Deterministic simulated-oracle results

The permanent Python standard-library tests independently evaluate the reviewed
monostatic phase law, explicit normalized sinc range response, coherent path
sum, private noise generator including MATLAB column-major layout,
half-power interpolation, partial-aperture cases, the complete seeded two-target
scene, and non-rigid path-error cases. They do not consume MATLAB output.

For `c=3e8 m/s`, `fc=5 GHz`, wavelength `0.06 m`, a 30 m aperture sampled
every `0.25 m`, a `990–1035 m` slant-range gate sampled every `0.5 m`, and a
`2.5 m` first-zero range-response scale:

- the deterministic collection contains 121 aperture looks by 91 complex
  range samples, and the 2-D image grid contains 121 by 65 = 7,865 pixels;
- two point targets are at `(-6, 1002) m` and `(7, 1020) m` with voltage
  amplitudes `1.0` and `0.75`;
- for 21, 61, and 121 centered looks, the independent target-1 full -3 dB
  cross-range widths are `5.075409018761`, `1.736000969436`, and
  `0.860332414164 m`, strictly decreasing;
- the corresponding noiseless unnormalized true-pixel voltages are exactly
  `21`, `61`, and `121`, demonstrating coherent voltage addition;
- in the complete seeded two-target scene, independent local searches place
  peaks exactly at `(-6, 1002) m` and `(7, 1020) m`, with respective focused
  voltages `120.564067597871` and `90.237275560464`;
- in the noiseless single-target path-sum oracle, 0, 5, and 10 mm sinusoidal
  assumed path errors give true-pixel coherent-gain ratios
  `1.000000000000`, `0.746208593722`, and `0.176717577908`, strictly
  decreasing below the `0.35` broken-case bound;
- with the complete seeded two-target measurement held fixed, the first
  target's corresponding ratios are `1.000000000000`, `0.745557432389`, and
  `0.175641598845`, while the second target's are `1.000000000000`,
  `0.745734527906`, and `0.175916405009`; both decrease strictly below the
  broken-case bound; and
- private seed 7701 begins with complex samples
  `1.651034520406-0.288082270375j` and
  `0.271098328070+0.116639757579j`; repeated calls match exactly and a changed
  seed differs.

Negative cases reject missing, empty, and placeholder artifacts; nested or
nonfinite vectors; unequal target-property lengths; invalid seeds/counts;

off-grid aperture/range endpoints; spatial aliasing; truncated target/image

## Residual risks

- MATLAB was not installed, so parser, local-function, numeric, assertion,
  runtime-duration, memory-inventory, and graphics behavior were assessed
  statically and with independent oracles rather than in the named runtime.
- Six figures were not rendered or manually reviewed; color contrast, peak
  visibility, legend placement, and learning effectiveness remain
  runtime/manual risks.
- The explicit pixel-look loops are intentionally transparent and bounded but
  have not been runtime benchmarked or interrupted with Ctrl+C.
- Linear interpolation is not a full fractional-delay filter. It makes
  mechanics visible but introduces small amplitude/phase approximation error.
- The normalized sinc is an idealized range-compressed response, not a retained
  P76 MATLAB output or a complete transmitted-waveform simulation.
- The constant-height discussion is intentionally qualified: in a flat 2-D
  straight-track model it may create coordinate bias rather than guaranteed
  blur. The reviewed defocus assertion applies to the named sinusoidal
  aperture-varying path error only.
- The model assumes stop-and-go collection, stationary isotropic point targets,
  exact nominal straight-track positions, narrowband carrier phase, no clutter,
  no propagation loss, and no extended terrain. P78 owns migration, P79 owns
  aperture/window resolution, and P80 owns motion-error autofocus.
