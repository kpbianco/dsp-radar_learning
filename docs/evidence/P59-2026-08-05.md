## Deterministic reference results

The independent Python oracle reproduces the script’s Park-Miller/Box-Muller
record, two-target scene, pair costs, greedy association, alpha-beta update,
truth-only scoring, and bounds.

- Seed-5908 position-only baseline: 24 wrong links and two identity transitions.
- Same reports with normalized velocity feature: zero wrong links and zero
  transitions in the reviewed baseline.
- Broken independent row minima: one report is reused on 12 scans, with 12
  wrong links and one transition.
- Recovery: assignment, position history, and velocity history exactly equal
  the reviewed velocity-aware result on identical input arrays.
- Reversing both report rows on every scan leaves assigned truth histories,
  position/velocity state histories, wrong-link counts, transition counts, and
  duplicate-use counts unchanged for both position-only and velocity-aware
  association. Report array position therefore does not act as target identity
  in the bounded oracle.
- Across seeds 5901:6100 at the nominal point, position-only association fails
  in 163/200 trials and the velocity-aware cost in 38/200. This synthetic
  reduction is not a universal or operational probability claim.

no MATLAB source repair was necessary.

## Residual risks

- MATLAB parsing, numerical execution, assertions, console output, and figure
  rendering remain unobserved because no MATLAB runtime is installed.
- The velocity report is an idealized Cartesian/Doppler-derived feature. A real
  radar may observe only radial velocity or a biased feature.
- Monte Carlo results cover a small declared model and seed family; they are
  deterministic educational examples, not calibrated fleet performance.
- The update-interval sweep direction combines wider physical spacing between
  adjacent scans with the smaller residual-driven correction `(beta/dt)r` at
  larger `dt`; maneuver/process uncertainty could reverse the tradeoff.
- Static tests cannot prove the lesson is pedagogically effective.
